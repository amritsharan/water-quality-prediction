WHO & EPA Water Quality Thresholds

Reference safe ranges established by the World Health Organization (WHO) and Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) for potable and aquatic water sources.

Parameter Safe Range Danger Threshold Impact of Exceedance
pH Level 6.5 – 8.5 < 6.5 or > 8.5 Corrosive water, metal toxicity leaching, digestive irritation.
Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) ≤ 500 mg/L > 500 mg/L Elevated salinity, mineral buildup, kidney load, unpleasant taste.
Turbidity ≤ 5.0 NTU > 5.0 NTU Pathogen protection shield, microbial contamination risk.
Dissolved Oxygen (DO) ≥ 7.0 mg/L < 4.0 mg/L Aquatic hypoxia, fish suffocations, anaerobic odor development.
Electrical Conductivity ≤ 800 μS/cm > 1000 μS/cm Indicates heavy ionic dissolved contaminants or runoff.
